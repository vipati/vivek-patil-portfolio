/ About

      

        
Twelve years of making systems measurably better.

        

          

            
I've spent most of my career on backend systems where small regressions reach a lot of people. At Microsoft I work on Office for the web (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, and Visio), owning performance, reliability, and the developer infrastructure behind them. That has meant caching and prefetching that halved load times, a release pipeline that ships in days instead of weeks, and production debugging that stays secure.

            
Lately I've been applying the same habits to LLM systems. I build gateways, agents, and guardrails the way I'd build any production service: measure first, cache only where it's safe, and fail closed when something looks wrong.

            
I lead a small group of three to four engineers, run design reviews, and care about leaving a team able to keep its systems healthy without me.
